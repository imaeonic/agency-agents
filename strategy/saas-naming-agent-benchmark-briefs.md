# SaaS Naming Strategist - Three-Brief Method Rehearsal

**Date:** 9 November 2026  
**Purpose:** Check that the revised agent's instructions can support genuinely different naming approaches and make rejection reasons explicit. This is a **desk-based specification rehearsal**, not an independent execution of the agent, live market survey, Namecheap check, legal clearance or human test. All sample names are **ILLUSTRATIVE / UNSCREENED** and may already be used.

## Common acceptance checks for each brief

1. State the intended buyer, user and AFTER without fabricating product details.
2. Create four independent territories (strategic, sound-first, morphology and distant metaphor).
3. Show the *actual* conceptual/phonological construction of each example.
4. Identify one phonetic or spelling failure instead of declaring all candidates excellent.
5. Give an unresolved screening/real-user test action; no claimed domain status.
6. Avoid a synthetic 100-point score.

## A. UK infrastructure monitoring SaaS

**Assumed brief:** A generic platform for IT teams monitoring infrastructure, with no confirmed features or monitoring outcomes beyond the category. **User/referrer:** network engineer. **AFTER (hypothesis):** can identify and discuss relevant monitoring events without name confusion. **Architecture:** broad master brand, not a feature name.

| Independent territory | Illustrative construction | Example | Risk / next test |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strategic: continuity / restoration | restoring operation → re-forming/weaving metaphor → re + loom | **Reloom** | Could be heard as "reloom"/"re-loom"; check other meanings and actual use |
| Sound-first | choose /ˈvɛ.lən/ → simple V-CV sequence → spell via common graphemes | **Velen** | May be transcribed Vellan or Vellin; hear→type test needed |
| Morphology / clipped blend | echo/trace → eco/echo sound + consonant insertion | **Echren** | Word-initial "ech" pronunciation uncertain; likely **reject** until first-sight test |
| Distant metaphor | stable route marker → real-word concept | **Cairn** | Very broad real word, high competitor/trade mark screening burden |

**Rehearsal observation:** Four distinguishable methods are represented. An agent that outputs only `signal-` or `pulse-` derivatives has failed the diversity criterion. No candidate is endorsed.

## B. Solo-professional appointment SaaS

**Assumed brief:** A self-employed professional uses the platform for managing appointments; avoid inventing channels, payments or integrations. **Buyer/user:** solo professional. **Referrer:** clients who mention the service. **AFTER (hypothesis):** easier to talk about their appointment arrangements.

| Independent territory | Illustrative construction | Example | Risk / next test |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strategic: coordination | shared schedule → alignment / meeting association | **Meetwell** | Looks like an existing English compound and may be overly descriptive; screening needed |
| Sound-first | choose /ˈta.və.loʊ/ → three simple syllables → Tavelo | **Tavelo** | Several hear-to-spell options (Tavello, Tavelow); **penalise** unless humans agree |
| Morphology / clipping | appointment → appoint → point → shortened recombination | **Pointa** | Ambiguous "point-ah" vs "poin-ta"; first-sight test needed |
| Distant metaphor | an available place/time as "a clearing" → nature metaphor | **Clearing** | Everyday word may be legally/SEO crowded; does not imply availability |

**Rehearsal observation:** The instinct to output generic `Bookly/Bookio/Slotify` derivatives is explicitly blocked. None of the above is a preferred final brand.

## C. Research-evidence workspace SaaS

**Assumed brief:** Organises research work and evidence for knowledge-focused teams; no unconfirmed AI or collaboration capabilities. **Buyer:** research lead. **AFTER (hypothesis):** easier to refer to the workspace and its supporting work.

| Independent territory | Illustrative construction | Example | Risk / next test |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strategic: synthesis | synthesis + credible extension → syn + vera | **Synvera** | `vera` associations and alternate spellings need checking; source report's example |
| Sound-first | /ˈluː.vən/ → CV.CVC → common spelling | **Luven** | Could be heard as Luvan or Looven; hear-to-type ambiguity |
| Morphology / recombination | evidence + weave → clipped roots → Eviweave | **Eviweave** | Awkward middle sound/forced blend; **reject** as a Frankenword |
| Contrarian metaphor | pieces of evidence → layered construction / real-world image | **Mosaic** | A common established word; possible many same-category conflicts; no search conducted |

**Rehearsal observation:** The agent should reject the invented compound even when its semantic rationale is neat, retain multiple directions for screening and favour user speech tests over a fabricated confidence rating.

## Rehearsal outcome and remaining validation

**Specification review: PASS with limitations.** The written process accommodates different naming families, true construction lineage, explicit rejection, phonetic caution, no default AI suffixes and a Namecheap-only availability boundary. These are checks of **instructions and worked examples**, not checks that a deployed agent consistently follows them.

**Not performed:** actual running of the agent across independent isolated contexts; competitor searches; Namecheap lookups; trademark searches; native-speaker checks; pronunciation/hear-to-spell testing; delayed recall; blind benchmark against the previous version.

**Before claiming proven naming improvement:** Execute identical real briefs through the original and enhanced prompts, blind the evaluators, test pronunciation and spoken spelling with target users, log rejected and differentiated surviving candidates, and compare actual counts and error rates. Keep outcomes per name and per territory, with no invented test statistics.
